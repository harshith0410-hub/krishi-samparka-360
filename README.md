1. PROJECT VISION

============================================================

Build a complete, modern, production-style agricultural web application called:

"ಕೃಷಿ ಸಂಪರ್ಕ 360 (Krishi Samparka 360)"

The application is designed primarily for farmers in Karnataka and should later be scalable across India.

The core objective is to create ONE simple platform where farmers can access:

• Nearby agricultural markets

• APMC markets

• Current crop prices

• AI crop price predictions

• Best market recommendations

• GPS-based market discovery

• Weather information

• Soil health information

• Fertilizer recommendations

• Crop planning

• Government schemes

• Agricultural equipment

• Agricultural labor

• Transport

• Warehouses

• Cold storage

• Buyers

• Voice assistance

• Notifications and alerts

The application should help farmers:

• Make better farming decisions

• Reduce unnecessary expenses

• Find better markets

• Reduce post-harvest losses

• Find agricultural services

• Improve farm profitability

• Access government information

• Understand weather conditions

• Make data-driven decisions

11. SMART MARKET RECOMMENDATION

============================================================

This is one of the MAIN INNOVATIONS.

Farmer enters:

• Location

• Crop

• Quantity

Example:

Location:

Mandya

Crop:

Tomato

Quantity:

500 kg

Search nearby markets.

For each market calculate:

• Current price

• Distance

• Estimated travel time

• Transport cost

• Market fee if available

• Demand

• Supply

• Gross revenue

• Estimated net revenue

Formula:

Gross Revenue =

Crop Price × Quantity

Net Revenue =

Gross Revenue - Transport Cost - Applicable Fees

Then rank markets.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT simply recommend the market with the highest crop price.

Recommend the market with the best estimated NET RETURN.

Example:

Mysuru APMC

₹26/kg

32 km

High demand

Gross revenue = ₹13,000

Transport = ₹1,200

Estimated net = ₹11,800

Mandya APMC

₹24/kg

14 km

Medium demand

Gross = ₹12,000

Transport = ₹500

Estimated net = ₹11,500

Yeshwanthpur APMC

₹29/kg

112 km

High demand

Gross = ₹14,500

Transport = ₹3,500

Estimated net = ₹11,000

Recommendation:

Mysuru APMC

Reason:

"After transportation costs, the estimated net revenue is highest."

• Recommended market
